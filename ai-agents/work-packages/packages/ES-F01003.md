# ES-F01003 — Spectate target protection revalidation

## 1. Package identity
`ES-F01003`; owner-authorized issue #216 repair for finding `R01-003`; internal `COMP-STAFF`; parallel-safe only while `StaffToolDispatcher` remains outside every other active package's exact changed paths.

## 2. Status
`ACTIVE` / `ACTIONABLE_CONTINUATION`.

## 3. Objective
Prevent a follow/spectate operation from continuing after its target becomes vanished or gains `enthusiastaff.stafftools.spectate-exempt` during asynchronous target/actor handoffs.

## 4. Why the package exists
Issue #216 finding `R01-003` proved that target protection was checked only during initial selection. The flow then used a location snapshot, teleported asynchronously, reacquired the target, and rechecked vanish but not the spectate-exempt permission before `setSpectatorTarget(...)`. A live permission change could therefore be ignored by an already-started privacy-sensitive operation.

## 5. Included finding
`R01-003` only.

## 6. Included behavior
Reacquire the live target on its owning scheduler before teleport admission, re-evaluate current vanish/exempt state there, recheck actor authority after the target-to-actor handoff, and re-evaluate current target protection again on the target scheduler immediately before direct spectator attachment. Retirement/disconnect or scheduler rejection remains fail-closed through the existing `onEntity(...)` retirement path.
