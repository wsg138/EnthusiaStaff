# Alt Investigations

Alt accounts are allowed on the Enthusia Network. Staff should investigate them
only when there is a moderation reason, such as punishment evasion, report abuse,
or another rule violation.

> **Current status:** The privacy and inheritance foundations exist, but the full
> alt commands, staff interface, evidence workflow, and staging verification are
> incomplete. Do not train staff to use commands that are not deployed.

## The main rule

A shared network does not prove that two accounts belong to the same person.
Families, roommates, schools, dorms, workplaces, mobile carriers, public Wi-Fi,
and VPN services can all produce legitimate overlap.

Do not punish a player only because:

- two accounts used the same network;
- usernames or skins look similar;
- their writing styles seem alike;
- they switched accounts quickly;
- another player claims they are the same person.

These may justify looking more closely, but they are not enough by themselves for
a severe action.

## Privacy

Staff should not need to see raw IP addresses. Use the approved alt tools and the
summary they provide.

Do not:

- ask another staff member to send you an IP address;
- paste addresses into case notes or Discord;
- keep a separate spreadsheet of network information;
- reveal network or household evidence to players.

## What to look for

Useful context may include:

- account-switching timing;
- whether both accounts play independently at the same time;
- shared or very different gameplay patterns;
- known family or household information already recorded by staff;
- repeated behavior connected to an active punishment;
- evidence from reports, chat, transactions, or server logs;
- a player openly confirming the relationship.

Independent simultaneous play is important evidence that two accounts may belong
to different people.

## Approved relationships

The system is intended to distinguish between:

- an allowed alt owned by the same player;
- separate people in a shared household;
- accounts staff have determined are not related;
- accounts strongly believed or confirmed to belong to the same person;
- weak or uncertain connections that should only create an alert.

Household, approved-alt, and not-related decisions should prevent automatic
punishment inheritance unless an authorized staff member reopens the decision
with new evidence.

## Punishment inheritance

When the system is fully deployed, a confidently related account may inherit the
remaining time of an active ban or mute. This is not supposed to create a new full
duration from the beginning.

Intentional punishment evasion is a separate decision. Do not add an evasion
punishment unless the evidence shows the player knowingly used another account to
avoid the active restriction.

## Investigation process

1. Start with the reason the accounts are being reviewed.
2. Read the available relationship summary and evidence.
3. Check for an existing household, approved-alt, or not-related decision.
4. Consider whether a restart, maintenance event, or mass reconnect could explain
   the timing.
5. Look for evidence that the accounts are used independently.
6. Separate automatic inheritance from intentional evasion.
7. Write a factual note explaining the decision.
8. Ask a Mod, Admin, or Founder before making a permanent relationship decision
   when the evidence is uncertain.

## Commands

The target design includes `/alts` and `/alt` commands. They are being developed
on the Velocity side and should not be treated as available until
[[Implementation Status]] and the live deployment confirm them.

## When to stop

Stop and ask for help when:

- the only evidence is network overlap;
- household members may be involved;
- accounts are playing independently at the same time;
- a permanent relationship label would be required;
- the inheritance result does not match the original punishment;
- private network information appears in a place staff should not see it.
